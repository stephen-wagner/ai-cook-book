# Soft White Sandwich Bread (Zojirushi BB-CEC20)

A soft white sandwich loaf for the Zojirushi BB-CEC20 bread machine, with all ingredients by weight. Blends all-purpose flour with Tipo 00 flour.

**Yield:** One 2 lb loaf

## Ingredients

All measurements in grams.

| Ingredient                         | Weight |
| ---------------------------------- | ------ |
| Water (cool)                       | 320 g  |
| Kirkland organic all-purpose flour | 408 g  |
| Polselli Tipo 00 flour             | 136 g  |
| Granulated white sugar             | 25 g   |
| Nonfat dry milk powder             | 16 g   |
| Fine table salt                    | 11 g   |
| Unsalted butter                    | 35 g   |
| Active dry yeast                   | 5.4 g  |

**Total ingredient weight:** 956.4 g before baking (approximately 2.11 lb of dough)

## Machine Settings

- **Machine:** Zojirushi BB-CEC20
- **Course:** BASIC
- **Loaf size:** 2 lb
- **Crust:** MEDIUM
- **Delay timer:** OFF

## Weighing Notes

- Use a digital kitchen scale accurate to 1 gram (0.1 g for yeast).
- Use fine table salt, not coarse kosher salt.
- Weigh the dry milk powder, not its volume.
- Use unsalted butter.

## Testing Notes

- Keep these quantities unchanged for the first test, so the result is controlled and shows whether the slightly sunken top has been corrected.
- The dry milk measurement was corrected in this version.
