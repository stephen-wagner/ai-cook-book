# 3-Ingredient Creamy Mac & Cheese

A stovetop mac and cheese made with only pasta, evaporated milk, and shredded cheese. No flour, roux, or butter required.

**Servings:** 4

## Ingredients

- 8 oz (225 g) elbow macaroni, or any short pasta
- 1 can (12 oz / 354 ml) evaporated milk
- 2 cups (about 8 oz / 225 g) shredded cheese

**Cheese:** Sharp cheddar (classic), Colby Jack (milder), or Monterey Jack (extra creamy). Shred your own if possible; pre-shredded cheese contains anti-caking agents that make it melt less smoothly.

## Method

1. **Cook the pasta.** Boil salted water, cook the pasta until just al dente, and drain well.
2. **Warm the milk.** Return the pot to low heat, add the evaporated milk, and warm for 1–2 minutes. Do not boil.
3. **Melt the cheese.** Add the shredded cheese a handful at a time, stirring constantly, until smooth. Stir the drained pasta back in until fully coated.

**If the sauce is too thick:** add a splash of hot water or more evaporated milk.
**If the sauce is too thin:** cook on low for 1–2 more minutes.

## Optional Additions

Salt, black pepper, garlic powder, paprika, or a small pat of butter for extra flavor.

## Notes

Evaporated milk contains concentrated milk proteins. When gently heated, they emulsify with melted cheese, creating a smooth sauce without flour or butter.
