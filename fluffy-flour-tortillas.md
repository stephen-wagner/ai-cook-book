# Homemade Fluffy Flour Tortillas

Soft, airy, pillowy flour tortillas like you get at a Mexican restaurant or tortillería. The secrets are the right fat, warm water, a well-rested dough, and not overcooking them.

**Makes:** 10–12 tortillas (about 8 inches)

## Ingredients

- 3 cups all-purpose flour (about 375 g)
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp salt
- ⅓ cup lard (or vegetable shortening)
- 1 cup warm water (105–110°F)
- 1–2 tbsp additional water, if needed

Lard gives the most authentic flavor and a particularly tender texture; shortening works too.

## Method

1. **Mix the dry ingredients.** In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, and salt.
2. **Work in the fat.** Add the lard and rub it into the flour with your fingers until it resembles coarse crumbs. This helps make a tender tortilla.
3. **Add warm water.** Gradually pour in the warm water while mixing, bringing it together into a dough. It should feel soft, smooth, and just slightly tacky, not sticky. If it feels dry or stiff, add a little more water.
4. **Knead.** Knead for about 4–5 minutes until smooth and elastic. Don't overwork it; you want a supple dough.
5. **Rest the dough (very important).** Divide into 10–12 equal balls. Lightly coat with oil, cover with a clean kitchen towel or plastic wrap, and rest for 30–45 minutes. This relaxes the gluten so the tortillas roll thin without shrinking back.
6. **Roll them out.** Roll each ball into a thin circle about 8 inches across. Keep the thickness even and avoid using too much flour on the surface.
7. **Cook.** Heat a cast-iron skillet or griddle over medium-high heat. Cook each tortilla:
   - First side: 20–30 seconds, until bubbles begin forming.
   - Flip: another 20–30 seconds.
   - Flip once more: 10–15 seconds if needed.

   You want light golden-brown spots, bubbles, and ideally a nice puff. Don't cook them until crispy.
8. **Keep them soft.** Immediately stack cooked tortillas in a clean kitchen towel and keep covered while cooking the rest. The trapped steam keeps them soft and pliable.

## Tips for Big, Fluffy Air Pockets

- **Use enough water.** A slightly softer dough makes a more tender tortilla.
- **Don't skip the rest.** This is one of the biggest secrets.
- **Roll evenly.** Thick and thin spots interfere with even puffing.
- **Get the skillet hot enough.** Too cool and the tortilla dries out before it puffs.
- **Don't overcook.** Too much time on the skillet makes tortillas tough and brittle.

Hydration, resting time, and skillet temperature matter most if you want them to balloon up almost like pita. Err toward a softer dough rather than a stiff one.

## Variation

- **Extra-tender:** Replace 2 tbsp of the water with 2 tbsp warm milk. It adds tenderness without making the tortillas taste like bread.
