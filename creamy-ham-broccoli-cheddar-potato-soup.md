# Creamy Ham, Broccoli & Cheddar Potato Soup

A rich, cheesy, creamy chowder with plenty of ham, tender potatoes, and broccoli in every bite — thickened with a light roux and mashed potatoes instead of blending.

**Servings:** 5–6
**Total time:** 50–55 minutes

## Ingredients

- 3 cups baked ham, diced
- 4 medium russet potatoes, peeled and diced (½-inch cubes)
- 3 cups fresh broccoli florets, chopped
- 1 medium yellow onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 3½ cups chicken broth
- 1½ cups heavy cream
- 3 tbsp butter
- 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 2½ cups freshly shredded extra-sharp cheddar
- ½ cup grated Parmesan
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- ¾ tsp black pepper
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp dried thyme
- ¼ tsp onion powder
- ⅛ tsp nutmeg (optional)
- Salt to taste

## Method

1. **Brown the ham.** Melt 1 tbsp butter in a large Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the ham and cook 5–6 minutes until lightly browned. Remove and set aside.
2. **Make the flavor base.** Add the remaining 2 tbsp butter to the pot. Sauté the onion 4–5 minutes, add the garlic and cook 30 seconds, then stir in the flour and cook 1 minute.
3. **Cook the potatoes.** Gradually stir in the chicken broth. Add the potatoes, black pepper, smoked paprika, thyme, and onion powder. Bring to a gentle boil, then reduce the heat and simmer 15–20 minutes until the potatoes are fork-tender.
4. **Add the broccoli.** Stir in the broccoli and simmer 4–6 minutes until tender but still holding its shape.
5. **Thicken and add cream.** Remove about 1½ cups of potatoes, mash thoroughly, and return them to the pot. Reduce the heat to low and stir in the heavy cream, Dijon mustard, and nutmeg (if using).
6. **Add ham and cheese.** Return the browned ham to the pot. Gradually stir in the cheddar, then the Parmesan. Stir gently over low heat until completely melted, about 3–5 minutes. Do not boil after adding the cheese.
7. **Final adjustments.** Taste and add salt if needed. If too thick, add warm chicken broth ¼ cup at a time. Let rest 5 minutes before serving.

## Tips

- Use freshly shredded extra-sharp cheddar for the smoothest melt.
- Mash some of the potatoes rather than blending the soup.
- Keep the heat low when adding the cheese to prevent a grainy texture.

## Serving Suggestions

Top each bowl with extra shredded cheddar, chopped green onions, fresh cracked black pepper, and crumbled bacon (optional).
