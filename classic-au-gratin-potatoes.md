# Classic Au Gratin Potatoes

A rich, creamy au gratin potato bake with a cheese sauce thickened with flour, layered with thinly sliced potatoes.

**Servings:** 6–8

## Ingredients

- 3 lbs Yukon Gold or russet potatoes, peeled and sliced ⅛-inch thick
- 3 tbsp butter
- 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 2 cups whole milk
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- ½ tsp onion powder
- ¼ tsp paprika (optional)
- 2 cups shredded sharp cheddar cheese
- 1 cup shredded Gruyère cheese (or substitute more cheddar)
- ¼ cup grated Parmesan cheese

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C). Butter a 9×13-inch baking dish.
2. In a saucepan over medium heat, melt the butter. Stir in the flour and cook for about 1 minute.
3. Slowly whisk in the milk and heavy cream until smooth. Add the garlic, salt, pepper, onion powder, and paprika. Simmer for 3–5 minutes until slightly thickened.
4. Remove from the heat and stir in the cheddar and Gruyère until melted.
5. Arrange half of the potato slices in the baking dish. Pour half of the cheese sauce over them. Repeat with the remaining potatoes and sauce.
6. Sprinkle the Parmesan evenly over the top.
7. Cover tightly with foil and bake for 45 minutes.
8. Remove the foil and bake for another 25–35 minutes, until the potatoes are tender and the top is golden brown.
9. Let the dish rest for 10–15 minutes before serving so the sauce thickens.

## Tips

- Use a mandoline slicer for evenly thin potato slices.
- For extra color, broil the potatoes for the last 2–3 minutes, watching carefully.
- This dish can be assembled a day ahead, refrigerated, and baked just before serving (add about 10–15 minutes to the baking time if baking straight from the refrigerator).

## Serving Suggestions

Pairs especially well with roast chicken, ham, steak, or holiday meals.
